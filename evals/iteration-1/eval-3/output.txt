Hook (0-2s):
Your morning coffee tastes bitter because the water is too hot, not because the beans are bad.

Story (2-13s):
Customers ask us every week which roast is least bitter. Then they go home and pour boiling water straight from the kettle onto the grounds, scorching them right away.

Turn (13-21s):
Water temperature matters just as much as the beans you buy. Even great coffee tastes harsh when boiling water burns it.

Takeaway (21-28s):
Tomorrow morning, take your kettle off the boil and wait thirty seconds before pouring.

Closer (28-30s):
Cool the water slightly, and taste the coffee you paid for.
