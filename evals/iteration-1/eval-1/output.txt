Hook (0-3s):
An open laptop in a small team meeting quietly kills real conversation.

Story (3-20s):
Last month, I stopped bringing my laptop into meetings with my small design team. Instead, I walked in with a single index card for notes. Nobody was answering Slack under the table anymore. People actually listened to each other, and our meetings got noticeably shorter.

Turn (20-32s):
A screen between you and your teammates splits everyone's attention in half. When you close the lid, people stop performing busyness and start solving the problem in the room.

Takeaway (32-42s):
For your next internal meeting, leave your laptop at your desk and bring one index card for notes instead.

Closer (42-45s):
One small card beats a room full of open screens.
