Hook (0-2s):
Your morning coffee tastes bitter because the water is too hot, not because the beans are bad.

Story (2-13s):
Customers ask us every week which roast is least bitter, then pour boiling water straight off the kettle and scorch the grounds.

Turn (13-21s):
Water temperature matters just as much as the beans you buy.

Takeaway (21-28s):
Take your kettle off the boil and wait thirty seconds before pouring.

Closer (28-30s):
Let the water cool slightly before you brew.
