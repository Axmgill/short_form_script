Hook (0-3s):
Open laptops in a small meeting kill real conversation before anyone speaks.

Story (3-20s):
Last month, I stopped bringing my laptop into meetings with my small design team. I walked in with a single index card for notes instead. Everyone stopped multitasking on Slack and paid attention to the room. Our meetings got noticeably shorter right away.

Turn (20-32s):
A screen between teammates splits everyone's focus in half. When laptops stay out of the room, people listen to each other and finish the conversation faster.

Takeaway (32-42s):
For your next internal meeting, leave your laptop at your desk and bring one index card instead.

Closer (42-45s):
Close the screen so the real meeting can start.
